Proxy definition

1,240 + (50,000 − 1,240) × local_mean / 100

The official score sums percentage-point improvements over 500 hidden cases. Its theoretical maximum is 50,000 under that convention.

The proxy anchors local v000 at its measured 1,240 score and maps a perfect local result to 50,000. It is useful for visual scale only.

It is not an official prediction. Organizer STD errors and hidden-case composition are unavailable.
